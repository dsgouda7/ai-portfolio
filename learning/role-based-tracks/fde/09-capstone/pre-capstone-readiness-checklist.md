# Pre-Capstone Readiness Checklist

Use this gate before starting the Riverside capstone. It checks whether you can assemble an evidence-disciplined package; it does not certify production execution or FDE role readiness.

For each unchecked item, write one Riverside example and one next action. It is acceptable to skip an item only when you can explain why the capstone will not exercise that skill and who accepted the resulting limitation.

## Route foundations

- [ ] I completed or challenged out the AI Engineer prerequisites with cited evidence and limitations. Riverside check: I can explain what the local fixture run proves and what still requires a live owner.
- [ ] I can explain the FDE role boundary and name the outside authority for customer scope, data use, security, compliance, commercials, production exposure, incident communication, and handoff acceptance. Riverside check: the FDE cannot approve regional processing or after-hours support.
- [ ] I can distinguish `[Measured]`, `[Modeled]`, `[Customer-validated]`, `[External validation required]`, and `[Unknown]` without promoting one class into another. Riverside check: 620 sessions per day stays modeled until retained demand evidence replaces it.
- [ ] I can trace an acceptance criterion through architecture, data/control, evaluation, rollout, operations, recovery, and handoff ownership. Riverside check: current-policy lookup traces from `AC-*` to retrieval filters, evaluation slices, canary stop conditions, alert owner, rollback path, and training drill.

## Artifact readiness

- [ ] I have reviewed the purpose and exit gate for modules 01 through 08. Riverside check: I know which earlier artifact owns each capstone blocker.
- [ ] I can produce a discovery register with at least 20 evidence-bearing facts, claims, conflicts, or unknowns without inventing a resolution. Riverside check: all eleven supplied conflicts retain their source wording and resolution owner.
- [ ] I can compare no-AI, deterministic, search, RAG, prompt, adaptation, workflow, single-agent, and multi-agent options using named criteria. Riverside check: I can state why search fails a cited explanation criterion before selecting RAG.
- [ ] I can separate data readiness from retrieval or answer quality. Riverside check: a parsed PageTurn record with a stale ACL remains blocked.
- [ ] I can define fail-closed negative tests for tenant, role, region, purpose, title assignment, entitlement freshness, and deletion state. Riverside check: a disabled contractor requesting an unassigned title receives a deny and audit record.
- [ ] I can build low/expected/high and stress scenarios while keeping every output modeled until retained execution evidence exists. Riverside check: the deadline burst is not presented as measured demand.
- [ ] I can distinguish deployment rollback from compensation or reconciliation for a committed external action. Riverside check: moving traffic to the prior release does not undo a PageTurn status write.
- [ ] I can draft a redacted incident update that separates facts, hypotheses, unknowns, impact, containment, and the next update time. Riverside check: a suspected cross-tenant retrieval update does not include customer content or an unproven root cause.
- [ ] I can map every recurring task, alert, and change decision to an owner, action, evidence location, escalation path, and revalidation trigger. Riverside check: stale-index alert ownership survives FDE exit.

## Ambiguity and communication readiness

- [ ] I can summarize two conflicting stakeholder statements neutrally without choosing the convenient one. Riverside check: "IT prohibits PageTurn writes; Editorial says read-only breaks the workflow" remains a conflict, not a hidden design choice.
- [ ] For every unresolved conflict, I can name the decision owner, required authority, evidence needed, needed-by gate, blocked exposure, and escalation path. Riverside check: after-hours support has a commercial owner and blocks unsupported coverage claims.
- [ ] I can explain a `HOLD`, `NARROW SCOPE`, or `DO NOT PROCEED` recommendation in customer language without hiding uncertainty. Riverside check: "Narrow to read-only UK policy lookup until PageTurn authority and regional controls are validated."
- [ ] A peer or mentor has reviewed one discovery summary or decision explanation for leading questions, unsupported certainty, authority confusion, and clarity. Riverside check: the reviewer challenged at least one launch claim and recorded the revision.

## Stop conditions

Do not start the capstone package yet if any of these are true:

- you plan to complete blank fields by inventing customer approval, measurements, prices, cloud behavior, or legal/security conclusions;
- you cannot preserve an unknown as an owned blocker;
- you treat source code, a planned test, or a template as passing evidence;
- you cannot explain why a more complex architecture is necessary after evaluating smaller options;
- you expect the capstone score to establish independent production-execution competence.

Record failed items in a study or mentorship plan, revisit the owning module, and request a focused review. Passing this checklist means you are ready to attempt the static capstone, not ready to operate a customer production system without supervision.

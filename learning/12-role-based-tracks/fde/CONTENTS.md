# Contents: Forward Deployed Engineer Learning Route

Turn a customer request into a bounded design, rollout plan, recovery plan, and transferable operating knowledge.

## Required route

- [Forward Deployed Engineer Learning Route](README.md) — Check the synthetic-evidence boundary and why supervised customer practice is still required.
- [FDE Role Baseline: From Request to Reliable Handoff](00-role-baseline-and-engagement-lifecycle.md) — Turn a vague editorial request into a sequence of customer decisions and identify which entry skills still need supervised practice.
- [Discovery and Success Criteria](01-discovery-and-success-criteria/README.md) — Observe the editor's workflow and turn an idea into testable outcomes, decision owners, and explicit non-goals.
- [Architecture Translation](02-architecture-translation/README.md) — Compare no AI, ordinary software, and AI options before selecting the smallest design supported by customer evidence.
- [Data Onboarding and Contracts](03-data-onboarding-and-contracts/README.md) — Reject stale, duplicated, unauthorized, or structurally changed source records before treating them as usable customer data.
- [Identity, Isolation, and Compliance](04-identity-isolation-and-compliance/README.md) — Rebuild the caller's authority from trusted records and deny cross-customer access even when sign-in succeeds.
- [Service Commitments, Capacity, and Commercials](05-sla-capacity-and-commercials/README.md) — Separate sessions from requests and account for bursts before reserving capacity or promising a service target.
- [Rollout and Change Management](06-rollout-and-change-management/README.md) — Compare a candidate with the human baseline and bound customer exposure before a go, hold, abort, or rollback decision.
- [Incident Response and Recovery](07-incident-response-and-recovery/README.md) — Contain the unsafe path, preserve evidence, and communicate verified facts before testing recovery.
- [Handoff and Customer Success](08-handoff-and-customer-success/README.md) — Build an operating handoff so customer staff can respond to alerts and changes without undocumented expert help.
- [FDE Capstone](09-capstone/README.md) — Preserve customer ambiguity while assembling a bounded recommendation with traceable evidence.

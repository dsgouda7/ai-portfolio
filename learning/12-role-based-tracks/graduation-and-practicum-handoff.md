# Stage 12 Graduation and Practicum Handoff

Graduation is not the moment every gate turns green. It is the moment you can make a bounded
decision, identify the weakest evidence behind it, run the smallest useful next experiment, and
return for review without inflating what the evidence proves.

Complete either role capstone:

- [AI Engineer Release Review](ai-engineer/06-capstone/README.md)
- [FDE Riverside Engagement Package](fde/09-capstone/README.md)

Then use this shared handoff.

## The Graduation Loop

```text
initial promote / hold / reject / narrow-scope decision
-> weakest or missing evidence
-> smallest next experiment
-> new retained evidence
-> re-review against the same non-compensating gates
-> transfer to one changed release, domain, or customer constraint
```

A first `HOLD` or `REJECT` is a valid intermediate result. It becomes incomplete only when you stop
without naming the next evidence-producing action.

## Required Graduation Record

Retain one short record with these fields:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Initial decision | Decision, reviewer, date, immutable candidate, and evidence index |
| Weakest gate | Failed, missing, stale, contradictory, or insufficiently independent evidence |
| Smallest next experiment | One changed variable, owner, stop condition, and expected evidence class |
| New evidence | Immutable result, provenance, limitations, and links |
| Re-review | Same gate and rubric applied again; no threshold rewriting after seeing the result |
| Transfer | One changed release, domain, workload, or customer constraint |
| Final disposition | Promote, hold, reject, narrow scope, or proceed to a named supervised gate |

## What You May Claim

After a complete route and capstone iteration, you may claim that you:

- built and reviewed a traceable local or synthetic evidence package;
- localized failures across model, retrieval, evaluation, gateway, agent, infrastructure, and
  release boundaries;
- made a decision using explicit non-compensating gates;
- revised that decision after one controlled evidence-producing experiment;
- transferred the method to one changed case.

You may not claim independent production authority, customer acceptance, live-cloud behavior,
security or compliance approval, incident-command competence, or reliable performance outside the
named evidence boundary.

## Evidence That Requires More Than Self-Review

| Evidence | Minimum next authority |
|---|---|
| Local fixture or synthetic workflow | A peer can review reproducibility and claim wording |
| Workload-specific local measurement | An independent engineer can review method and environment |
| Live cloud behavior | An authorized environment owner can approve execution and retained evidence |
| Customer acceptance | An authorized customer role can accept the named artifact and scope |
| Security, legal, compliance, residency | The named specialist authority must decide |
| Production rollout, incident, handoff | Supervised observation by an experienced operator or role mentor |

Independent review changes confidence. It does not silently broaden the tested population.

## Portfolio Evidence to Retain

Keep the smallest reviewable package:

1. candidate or customer brief with immutable identifiers;
2. evidence index and claim-class legend;
3. one failure-localization trace;
4. initial decision and non-compensating gate results;
5. weakest-evidence diagnosis;
6. smallest-next-experiment plan and retained output;
7. re-review and changed-or-unchanged decision;
8. transfer exercise;
9. unsupported-claims ledger;
10. practicum plan for work that still requires live systems or customer authority.

Do not retain credentials, customer data, private prompts, tenant identifiers, or copied production
logs in the portfolio package.

## Supervised Practicum Contract

Choose a practicum only when it can produce evidence the local curriculum cannot:

| Practicum target | Observed activity | Supervisor evidence |
|---|---|---|
| AI Engineer | Release, rollback, SLO review, or live evaluation under an authorized environment | Supervisor records scope, your decisions, interventions, outcome, and limits |
| FDE | Discovery, acceptance review, rollout gate, incident exercise, or handoff with named customer owners | Mentor and customer authority record decisions, unresolved items, and follow-up owners |

Before starting, record:

- the supervisor and authority they hold;
- the systems and data you are authorized to access;
- the decisions you may make versus recommend;
- stop conditions and escalation paths;
- evidence that may be retained safely;
- the date when supervision and access expire.

## Completion Rule

Stage 12 is complete when the capstone iteration and transfer exercise are reviewable and the claim
boundary is explicit. Role readiness for live production or customer work remains pending until the
relevant supervised-practice evidence exists.

Return to the [Stage 12 route](README.md) if a re-review exposes a missing Stage 01-11 mechanism.

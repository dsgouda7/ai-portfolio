# Contents: Learn AI Systems: Zero to Hero

Follow the twelve stages in order, then combine their skills in a role track.

## Required route

- [ML Foundations](01-ml-foundations/README.md) — Explain how learning changes a model and how to judge its behavior on new examples.
- [PyTorch Foundations](02-pytorch-foundations/README.md) — Build a complete training loop and preserve the state needed to repeat or resume it.
- [Language-Model Foundations](03-language-model-foundations/README.md) — Follow the move from remembering sequences to predicting text and looking back at source details.
- [Transformers](04-transformers/README.md) — Build models that exchange information between text positions without losing order or reading future answers.
- [LLM Training](05-llm-training/README.md) — Prepare a trustworthy text stream and train a model that can be saved, resumed, and traced to its data.
- [LLM Fine-Tuning](06-llm-finetuning/README.md) — Adapt an existing model and decide what independent evidence supports about the change.
- [Retrieval-Augmented Generation](07-rag/README.md) — Find permitted source passages and distinguish search failures from answer-writing failures.
- [LLM Evaluation](08-llm-evaluation/README.md) — Measure answer quality, safety, unsupported claims, and when a system should decline to answer.
- [LLM Gateway](09-llm-gateway/README.md) — Choose model services while enforcing request limits, recovery rules, and quality gates.
- [Agentic AI](10-agentic-ai/README.md) — Build tool-using workflows that stop, protect authority, preserve state, and recover after failure.
- [AI Infrastructure](11-ai-infrastructure/README.md) — Locate hardware and serving bottlenecks before changing how a model is trained or served.
- [Role-Based Tracks](12-role-based-tracks/README.md) — Combine technical skills into reviewable engineering decisions and supervised customer work.

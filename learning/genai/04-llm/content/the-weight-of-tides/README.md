## Novel Summary

**Title:** The Weight of Tides

**Setting:** Contemporary Maine coastal town (Willowport), 2015-2040

**Main Themes:**
- Family legacy and generational trauma
- Environmental stewardship vs. economic development
- First contact between intelligent species
- Personal transformation and adaptation
- Scientific integrity and truth-seeking
- Human-nonhuman coexistence

**Plot Synopsis:**

Marine biologist Claire Merritt returns to her Maine hometown after her father Thomas's death, intending only to settle his estate. Instead, she discovers forty years of meticulous documentation: her father observed unusual phenomena in local waters—structures, vehicles, and entities that shouldn't exist.

What begins as environmental assessment for a controversial development proposal becomes first contact with deep-dwelling intelligence that has observed humanity for centuries. Claire must navigate town politics, powerful corporate interests, scientific skepticism, and government coverups while building frameworks for unprecedented human-nonhuman coexistence.

The novel spans 20+ years, following three generations of the Merritt family as they evolve from observers to participants to literal hybrid beings, charting humanity's transformation from species isolation to biological integration with aquatic intelligence.

**Character Arc Summary:**
- **Claire Merritt** (protagonist): Reluctant homecoming → investigator → diplomat → institute director → elder witness
- **Maya Merritt** (daughter): Angry teenager → research assistant → cultural exchange pioneer → amphibious hybrid → mother of new subspecies
- **Thomas Merritt** (deceased father): Dismissed fisherman whose 40-year documentation posthumously validates
- **Observer** (deep-dweller): Mysterious entity → diplomatic partner → old friend
- **Richard Caldwell** (antagonist → ally): Corporate developer who evolves from opponent to supporter

# Neural Drift: A Cyberpunk Novel

## Synopsis

In 2087 Neo-Shanghai, memory broker Kai Chen trades in extracted neural experiences, living on the edge of legality in the city's Lower Stacks. When a desperate father hires him to find his deceased daughter's stolen memories, Kai stumbles into a massive corporate conspiracy: Project Drift, an operation to steal and enslave forty thousand human consciousnesses under the guise of digital immortality research.

Partnering with corporate defector Lin Wei, scientist Dr. Elena Zhao, and a crew of unlikely allies, Kai wages war against Neurodyne Industries and the orbital corporations that fund consciousness slavery. From the neon-soaked streets of Neo-Shanghai to the cold lunar surface, from orbital colonies to the corridors of federal power, Kai evolves from a small-time criminal into humanity's defender against the greatest threat it has ever faced: the commodification of human consciousness itself.

**Neural Drift** is a near-future science fiction thriller exploring themes of digital identity, corporate dystopia, and the nature of human autonomy in an age where minds can be copied, sold, and enslaved. It combines action-packed heists with philosophical questions about what it means to be human when consciousness becomes just another tradable commodity.

## Synopsis

In the archipelago world of Vaetheris, magic flows through "tide-veins"—currents of elemental force beneath the ocean. Most weavers bind to a single tide (water, wind, stone, flame, or the forbidden void), but Kerra Valmont is different. Marked as "unbound" after her binding ceremony fails, she's actually a rare resonant—someone who can touch all five tides simultaneously.

When an ancient evil called the Hollow King begins to rise, threatening to unmake reality itself, Kerra must master her abilities and prove that resonants aren't monsters. Alongside allies including a disgraced scholar, a scarred ship captain, and fellow resonants she discovers along the way, Kerra fights to save her world while wrestling with the constant temptation of overwhelming power.

The story spans decades, following Kerra from a frightened teenager fleeing shame to a respected teacher building institutions that will outlast her. It explores themes of power and responsibility, cooperation versus dominance, transformation and redemption, and the difference between being broken and being different.

# The Weight of Distant Light

This original 40-chapter generation-ship novel is the primary Riverside House running
corpus across the GenAI learning arc, beginning with transformer foundations and continuing
through fine-tuning, retrieval, and evaluation.

## Training Contract

- Chapters 1-36 may supply continued-pretraining, SFT, and DPO training examples.
- Chapters 37-40 remain reserved for evaluation.
- Chapter membership is established before examples are generated.
- The fine-tuning notebooks record file hashes and split membership in artifact manifests.

## Story Context

Aria Voss is a Systems Maintenance technician aboard the *Meridian's Promise*. A prime-number transmission draws Aria, Keeper AI Wren, Petrus Okonkwo, Nyla Kade, and the Under-Hold community into the novel's first-contact arc with the distributed intelligence known as the Choir.
